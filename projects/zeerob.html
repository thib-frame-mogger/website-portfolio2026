Zeerob Zeerob

Zeerob is a sea kayak currently in development.

Manufacturing the prototype of a 3.7m long kayak fully made of aluminium in a 4m long workshop is a challenge itself.
The project aims to developp the lightest kayak possible while keeping the structural integrity of the hull and the comfort of the cockpit, accounting for buoyancy, stability and hydrodynamics.
