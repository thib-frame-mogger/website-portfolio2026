Background

I am an emerging French designer/artisan. I graduated from the ENSAAMA in Paris with a speciality in metal creation and I'm currently studying at Nancy École des Minesin a Master of design focused on product conception. My workflow integrates both traditional artisanship and digital conception. Being originally from Lille, I find inspiration in the northern French culture and its folkloric heritage. Parcours

Je suis designer/artisan français émergent.
